# Contact Template — First message to an agent

Use this when reaching out about a listing the user has decided is worth pursuing (score ≥ 4.0).

## Principles

1. **Short.** 4-6 sentences max. Agents read dozens of these a day.
2. **Specific.** Reference the listing reference number or address. Shows you read it.
3. **Qualified.** Mention financing status and timeline up front. Agents prioritize ready buyers.
4. **Two concrete asks.** Visit window + one specific question. Don't ask 5 things.
5. **No fluff.** No "I'm very interested in your beautiful property". Skip directly to the ask.
6. **Match the channel.** Portal contact forms → keep it tight. Email → can add 1-2 lines of context. WhatsApp → even tighter.

## Template (Portuguese)

```
Bom dia [agente],

Vi o seu anúncio do [T2/T3 em Alvalade, ref. XXXXX] e gostaria de marcar uma visita.

Sou comprador [com financiamento pré-aprovado / a pagar a pronto], pretendo escriturar até [mês/ano], e estou a procurar nesta zona há [tempo].

Disponibilidade para visita: [dias e janelas horárias concretas].

Uma questão antes da visita: [a única pergunta crítica — ex: ano de remodelação / orientação solar / certificado energético / se o condomínio tem obras previstas].

Obrigado,
[Nome]
[Telemóvel]
```

## Template (English)

```
Hi [agent],

I saw your listing for [the T2 in Alvalade, ref. XXXXX] and would like to book a viewing.

I'm a [pre-approved / cash] buyer, planning to close by [month/year], and have been searching in this area for [time].

I'm available [specific day/time windows].

One question before the visit: [the single most important question — e.g. year of last renovation, sun orientation, energy certificate, planned condo works].

Thank you,
[Name]
[Phone]
```

## Adapting

- **Hot listing (priced below market, will move fast):** lead with the visit request, ask the question after the visit.
- **Listing more than 60 days old:** can be more relaxed; ask if there's flexibility on price up front.
- **Agency vs owner:** with an owner, skip the "I'm pre-approved" framing — they want a person, not a profile.
- **Multiple listings with same agent:** mention 2 at most, ask which is the better fit for you given your criteria.

## What NOT to do

- Don't disclose your max budget. Stay vague: "around your asking price" or "in your range".
- Don't beg or oversell. Confidence wins.
- Don't ask for the floor plan or full document set before the visit. Wait.
- Don't share your full financing details (LTV, bank). The agent doesn't need it yet.
