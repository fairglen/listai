# Offer Letter Template (Portugal)

Use after a visit, when the user has decided to make an offer.

**Process note:** In Portugal a purchase offer is non-binding until a CPCV (Contrato Promessa de Compra e Venda) is signed with a sinal (deposit). The first written offer is typically by email; if accepted, both sides move to CPCV within 2-4 weeks.

## Principles

1. **Anchor low but not insulting.** First offer at 90-95% of asking, with documented reason.
2. **Justify with data.** €/m² of comparables, days on market, condition issues observed during visit.
3. **Add terms the seller wants.** Fast closing date, clean financing, no contingencies = leverage.
4. **Set expiration.** 48-72h response window prevents drift and shopping.
5. **No emotion.** This is a contract negotiation, not a love letter.

## Template (Portuguese)

```
Assunto: Proposta de compra — [endereço ou ref. do anúncio]

Bom dia [agente / vendedor],

Após visita ao imóvel em [data], apresento a seguinte proposta de compra:

VALOR PROPOSTO: € [valor] (em vez de € [asking])
JUSTIFICAÇÃO:
- €/m² do imóvel: € [calc]/m² (área útil [X]m²)
- Comparáveis na zona: € [Y]-[Z]/m² em [referências concretas: ano, ref. de anúncio ou venda recente]
- [Aspectos observados na visita que justificam ajuste: ex. caixilharia a substituir, casa de banho por remodelar, exposição menos favorável]
- [Dias no mercado, se relevante]

CONDIÇÕES:
- Financiamento: [pré-aprovado pelo banco X / pronto pagamento]
- Sinal: [% do valor, tipicamente 10%] em CPCV no prazo de [N] dias após aceitação
- Escritura: até [data]
- Sem outras condições suspensivas

Esta proposta é válida até [data + 48-72h, 18h00].

Aguardo a sua resposta.

Cumprimentos,
[Nome]
[Telemóvel]
[Email]
```

## Template (English)

```
Subject: Purchase offer — [address or listing ref.]

Hi [agent / owner],

Following the visit on [date], please find my purchase offer below:

OFFERED PRICE: € [value] (vs asking € [asking])
REASONING:
- Property €/m²: € [calc]/m² (useful area [X]m²)
- Area comparables: € [Y]-[Z]/m² ([cite refs: year, listing ref or recent sale])
- [Observed issues that justify the adjustment: e.g. windows to replace, bathroom dated, lower sun exposure]
- [Days on market, if relevant]

TERMS:
- Financing: [pre-approved with Bank X / cash]
- Deposit: [% of price, typically 10%] via CPCV within [N] days of acceptance
- Deed (escritura) signing: by [date]
- No additional suspensive conditions

This offer is valid until [date + 48-72h, 6:00 PM].

Looking forward to your reply.

Best,
[Name]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

## Counter-offer playbook

| Situation | Move |
|-----------|------|
| Seller counters above your max | Reply same day with revised offer at midpoint + 1 specific reason for the cap |
| Seller refuses to move | Ask what would make a deal — terms, closing date, included furniture — sometimes the gap closes without price |
| Seller says "another offer is on the table" | Ask for a deadline. If real, decide your walk-away in advance. Often it's a tactic. |
| Seller accepts immediately | You probably overpaid. Don't celebrate yet — finish due diligence (legal, structural) before signing CPCV |

## Pre-CPCV checklist (NEVER skip)

Before signing CPCV with the sinal:

- [ ] Caderneta Predial (tax record matches the property)
- [ ] Certidão Predial Permanente from Predial Online (clean title, no ónus/hipotecas)
- [ ] Licença de Utilização (use license — required, may not exist for old buildings)
- [ ] Certificado Energético (energy cert, mandatory for sale)
- [ ] Ficha Técnica de Habitação if built after 2004
- [ ] Condomínio: quotas in good standing, no special assessments pending, minutes of last 2 assemblies
- [ ] IMI status (no debt)
- [ ] If new construction: certificate of habitation
- [ ] If pre-1951 or in historic center: heritage classification, occupancy rules
- [ ] Mortgage formal approval letter from bank (not just pre-approval)

## What NOT to do

- Don't sign CPCV the same day as accepting the offer. 2-week minimum for due diligence.
- Don't pay the sinal in cash. Bank transfer with clear reference.
- Don't accept "the owner doesn't have the energy certificate yet" — by law they must provide it before signing.
- Don't waive the financing contingency unless you're a cash buyer.
- Don't sign anything in English only — the binding version is Portuguese.
